2027 Programme Structure

Comprehensive Theory Meets High-Access Dissection

Designed for ENT consultants, fellows, trainees, international delegates, and speech & language therapists. Dissectors rotate in small groups of six to guarantee maximum hands-on surgical time.

Day 1 • Thursday 1 July 2027

Didactic Lectures & Challenging Case Panels

Focused, clinically relevant talks delivered by national and international experts, covering contemporary diagnostic and operative decision-making.

  • Assessment & management of complex voice, airway, and swallowing disorders
  • Setting up modern multidisciplinary voice & TNO clinics
  • Surgical frameworks: laryngeal reinnervation, medialisation thyroplasty & airway stenosis
  • Interactive multidisciplinary case discussion panels & laser safety certification
Day 2 • Friday 2 July 2027

Supervised Hands-On Surgical Rotations

Dissectors are divided into four groups of six, rotating through four dedicated 60-minute practical stations with 1:3 faculty supervision.

  • 10–15 minute structured faculty workflow demonstration per station
  • 45–50 minutes of direct, hands-on delegate practice on porcine & simulation models
  • Parallel dissection tables and dual CO₂ laser setups to eliminate waiting times
  • Observers receive full access to Day 1 lectures and Day 2 station demonstrations
Station 01

Injection Laryngoplasty

Peroral and transcutaneous injection techniques, plane depth, and augmentation strategy on specialized laryngeal simulation models.

Station 02

VOIS Thyroplasty

External framework landmarking, thyroid cartilage window creation, and VOIS implant sizing & placement on porcine larynges.

Station 03

TNO & Blue Laser

Channel-scope transnasal oesophagoscopy, guidewire balloon dilatation, and ARC Blue Laser ablation of vocal fold lesions.

Station 04

CO₂ Laser & VITOM

Microlaryngoscopy laser handling, cordectomy, and partial arytenoidectomy using KARL STORZ VITOM exoscope systems.
